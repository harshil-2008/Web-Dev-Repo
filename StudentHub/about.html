About StudentHub

StudentHub is a modern, feature-rich student portal designed to bridge the gap between students, faculty, and administration. Our platform provides a centralized hub for all academic activities, making education management seamless and efficient.

Built with students in mind, StudentHub simplifies daily academic tasks — from tracking attendance and submitting assignments to viewing grades and downloading study materials.

Our Mission

To empower students with a digital-first academic experience that is intuitive, accessible, and efficient. We believe technology should simplify education, not complicate it.

Objectives

  • ✓ Provide real-time access to attendance records and academic performance metrics
  • ✓ Enable seamless online assignment submission and tracking
  • ✓ Centralize study material distribution from faculty to students
  • ✓ Offer transparent grade reports with GPA and CGPA calculations
  • ✓ Foster better communication between students and institution
  • ✓ Ensure mobile-friendly, accessible design for all users
