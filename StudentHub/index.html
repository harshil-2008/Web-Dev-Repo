Why StudentHub?

StudentHub is a comprehensive student management portal designed to streamline your academic life. Access your attendance records, submit assignments, check grades, and download study materials — all from a single platform.

Features

📋

Attendance Tracking

View your subject-wise attendance records, track your overall attendance percentage, and stay on top of your presence in every class.

📝

Assignment Management

Submit assignments online, track due dates, and check submission status. Never miss a deadline again with our smart reminder system.

📊

Grade Reports

Access your subject-wise marks, GPA, and CGPA with beautiful visualizations. Monitor your academic performance in real-time.

📚

Study Materials

Download lecture notes, PDFs, PPTs, and other study resources shared by your faculty. Search by subject or topic for quick access.
