About StudentHub

StudentHub is a modern student portal designed to simplify campus life. Students can register for events, manage their academic profile, receive important announcements, join clubs, and stay connected with their college community through one centralized platform.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide every student with a simple and efficient platform for academic management and campus engagement.

🌍 Our Vision

To create a connected campus where information, communication, and opportunities are accessible to everyone.

🚀 Features

• Event Registration
• Student Dashboard
• Announcements
• Clubs & Activities
• Feedback System
